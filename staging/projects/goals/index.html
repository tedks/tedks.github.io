goals
Literally addictive task-tracking
2026-04-01
  • goalsapp.org
A goal tracking app that gets you literally addicted to being productive. Goals works via variable-schedule operant conditioning. You have a “streak,” but you don’t know when it will end - it might be at the next task, it might be tomorrow. This is the same cognitive mechanism as slot machines. Goals supports traditional tasks, repeating tasks, habits, and directed acyclic connections between tasks. Runs fully locally with a paid upgrade for serverside sync.
